## ▶ 2026-08-20 — POSITIONING: say "by car enthusiasts", not just "for car enthusiasts"

**Tre's ask, verbatim:** *"we need to advertise this more as a budgeting app made for car
enthusiasts by car enthusiasts."*

**Treat this as the headline claim from now on, not a tagline option.** Every campaign in
`PLAN.md` already leads with the car — the plan's own wedge line is *"The wedge is not budgeting.
The wedge is the build."* What it does NOT say anywhere is who built it. That second half is the
part being added.

### Why the "by" half is the one that does the work

The audience is defined in `PLAN.md` as hostile to anything that reads as an ad and generous to
anyone who does real math for them in public. For that audience:

- **"For car enthusiasts" is a market segment.** Any budgeting app can bolt on a vehicle tab and
  claim it. It is a positioning statement, and positioning statements are what ads sound like.
- **"By car enthusiasts" is a credential.** It cannot be bolted on, it is the reason the build
  log, the per-part budget, the insurance shock and the loan engine all exist in one app, and it
  is the answer to the only question that sub actually asks a new tool: *does this person
  understand my problem or are they selling to me?*

It also explains the product honestly. The features that make Forgenta different from Mint or
YNAB are not a segment strategy — they exist because the person who built it needed them.

### How to apply it

- **Lead with it in bios and first lines**, everywhere there is a bio: Reddit profile, the
  Instagram/TikTok bio, the email footer, the `/answers/` page boilerplate, the app store
  descriptions. Shortest usable form: *"A budgeting app for car people, built by one."*
- **Campaign 2 (Project Ledger) is the proof, so stop treating it as one campaign of six.** A
  public build thread with real numbers IS the "by car enthusiasts" claim being demonstrated
  rather than asserted. If effort has to be cut somewhere, cut elsewhere.
- **Campaign 1 (Pit Crew) already handles the disclosure rules** — see
  `marketing_reddit_scout` and the per-subreddit policy. The "by" framing changes the wording of
  the disclosure, not whether it happens: *"I built this because I needed it"* is both the
  positioning and an honest affiliation disclosure in one sentence.
- **Campaign 3/6 (video, carousels):** the founder's own car and its real numbers on screen beat
  a UI walkthrough. Keep using real figures from demo mode (`capture_demo.mjs`) so nothing
  personal ships, but the STORY should be first-person.
- **Do not let it drift into "not a real budgeting app".** It is a full personal-finance app; the
  car is the door in, not the ceiling. Anyone arriving for the build log should find the
  forecast, the debt engine and the goals waiting.

### Still open

- The app store descriptions and the site's own meta copy have **not** been rewritten against
  this. That is the highest-leverage unshipped piece — it is the one surface where the claim is
  read by people who did not come from a campaign.
- No decision yet on whether the founder is named/shown. The claim is stronger with a face, and
  that is Tre's call, not a marketing default. **Ask before putting his name or car in a public
  asset.**
